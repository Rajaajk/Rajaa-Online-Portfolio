Approach

How I Build Products for Humans

Constraints aren't limitations — they're innovation catalysts. In markets where every tap costs data and every second matters, simplicity isn't optional.

01

Start with Access

If users can't reach it, it doesn't exist. Every product begins with the question: how do we get this into their hands with zero friction?

02

Design for Friction

Every interaction costs data, time, and trust. Make every tap count. Strip away everything that doesn't serve the user's core need.

03

Measure Behavior

In low-trust markets, actions speak louder than surveys. Watch what users do, not what they say. Let data drive every iteration.

01 · AI · Zong

OzGPT — Multilingual AI for Feature Phones

Pakistan's first telco AI assistant, supporting 5 regional languages across 5 access channels.

563%MAU Growth
215KUsers (3.5 wks)
13.6%Peak Stickiness

My Role

  • Multi-channel accessibility requirements (USSD, SMS, IVR, web, app)
  • Curated multilingual datasets (Urdu, Punjabi, Sindhi, Pashto, English)
  • Telco billing & CRM integration coordination
  • Voice-first UX optimization for low-literacy users

Key Decisions

  • USSD first: Launched *6131# before Play Store
  • Regional language fine-tuning: LLM adapted for local dialects
  • IVR → SMS fallback: Zero query drop-off
  • Data pack bundles: Integrated with daily/weekly plans

Global Relevance

Multilingual, low-bandwidth AI distribution is critical for reaching 2.8B+ non-English speakers. Skills transfer directly to MENA, Africa, and LATAM markets.
